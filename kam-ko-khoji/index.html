Stay safe सावधान रहनुहोस्

Never pay for a job

काम पाउन कसैलाई पैसा नतिर्नुहोस्। A real employer does not ask workers for money for a job, a contract or a "visa file".

Keep your passport

आफ्नो पासपोर्ट कसैलाई नदिनुहोस्। Show a copy only. No one is allowed to keep your original passport or TRC card.

Get a written contract

लिखित सम्झौता लिनुहोस्। Before you start, sign an umowa and keep a copy. Ask for it in English if you don't read Polish.

Report bad jobs

शंकास्पद काम देख्नुभयो? Use the "Report" button on any job and our team will check it.

Privacy · गोपनीयता

  • We collect only what you type in the forms and the documents you upload.
  • Documents are stored in a private, encrypted storage. They are never shown on the website.
  • Your application is shared only with the employer of the job you applied to.
  • Applications and documents are deleted after 90 days, or earlier if you ask.
  • To see or delete your data, contact us using the details at the bottom of the page.
